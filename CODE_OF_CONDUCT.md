# Bilgin Apps Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Bilgin Apps aims to foster a respectful, inclusive, collaborative environment for people who design, build, maintain, and contribute to our learning products.

This code applies to issue discussions, pull requests, code reviews, project planning, and other spaces managed by the organization. It also applies when someone officially represents Bilgin Apps.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, welcoming, and constructive, especially when opinions differ.
- Assume good intent while remaining open to feedback about impact.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not the people proposing them.
- Communicate clearly, patiently, and professionally.
- Respect differences in experience, background, identity, ability, and perspective.
- Protect the privacy and safety of learners, families, colleagues, and contributors.
- Give credit for others' work and acknowledge mistakes openly.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not tolerated:

- Harassment, bullying, intimidation, threats, or discriminatory remarks.
- Sexualized language, unwelcome advances, or unwanted personal attention.
- Publishing or threatening to publish another person's private information.
- Sharing learner, child, customer, or colleague data without appropriate authorization.
- Deliberate disruption, personal attacks, or sustained bad-faith conduct.
- Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith.

## Raising concerns

If a discussion becomes uncomfortable or unsafe, stop engaging and contact an organization owner through an established **private** communication channel. Please include relevant links, context, and evidence while avoiding unnecessary sharing of personal data.

For software vulnerabilities, use the separate [Security Policy](SECURITY.md), not a public issue or code-of-conduct report.

## Review and response

Organization owners will assess reports with discretion, take reasonable steps to protect affected people, and determine an appropriate response. Responses can include a request to change behavior, removing harmful content, restricting participation, or revoking access.

We cannot guarantee a specific response timeframe or absolute confidentiality, but reports will be handled with care and information shared only as needed for review and action.

## Scope and updates

This policy provides a baseline for Bilgin Apps spaces. Repository-specific guidelines may provide additional expectations, provided they do not reduce the protections described here.

We may update this policy as the organization and its community grow.
